How it works

  1. 1

    Load

    The cashier taps the ID and mints credits from MetaMask. The top-up appears on the explorer.

  2. 2

    Pay offline

    At the stall: tap the ID, confirm, enter the PIN. The stall shows a receipt QR for the student’s phone.

  3. 3

    Settle

    Whichever phone gets signal first sends the receipt. The contract settles it once and ignores the copy.

What keeps offline payments safe

  • tune

    A personal offline limit

    Each student gets an offline cap from their top-ups and payment history, instead of a flat 200 pesos.

  • flag

    Clone detection

    The same ID at two stalls minutes apart, odd amounts, or bursts of small payments get flagged. A flagged ID drops to a 0 peso offline cap.

  • smart_toy

    A spending assistant

    Students ask what they spent this week and get an answer built only from their own receipts.

Known limits

  • ID chips can be cloned.

    The PIN, offline caps and clone detection limit the damage. Anti-clone IDs are on the roadmap.

  • Two offline stalls could spend the same last pesos.

    The contract rejects the overdraft at settlement and that offline cap drops to 0.

  • A receipt settles only when a phone gets signal.

    Both phones carry it. A Bluetooth relay between phones is the roadmap answer.

  • Testnet only.

    No real money. A pilot needs university approval and BSP review.

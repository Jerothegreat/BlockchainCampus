# Design System: HeronPay

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Printed Proof"** (proposed)

The receipt is the product's proof, so HeronPay draws every payment as the subject's own artifact: a torn thermal-paper slip laid on a deep navy counter at night. Approved prints the slip from the top down; Settled stamps it with the block number. The slip is the one warm, paper-coloured thing on screen, which is why it reads as the object of record and everything around it reads as the machine.

Structure is Material 3 at default density (top app bar 64, navigation bar 80, list items 72), so the app feels like a well-behaved Android citizen; the look comes from the palette, the slip and the stamp, not from bending M3. Gold is the one action colour and appears where a thumb should go. Amounts, receipt ids, hashes and student numbers are set in JetBrains Mono because they are data, the same way a till prints them. Dark is the default theme; light is fully designed, with white slips on a cool grey ground.

The world refuses the generic wallet-card dashboard: no faux bank card, no gradient balance hero. The balance is a plain tonal card; the payment is a slip.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Deep navy ground, warm paper slips, one gold action colour.
- Thermal receipt slip with zig-zag torn edges, mono data rows and a TOTAL line as the signature object.
- Rubber stamps (SETTLED green with block number, PENDING blue) in reserved space at the slip foot.
- Material 3 components and type roles at default density; tonal elevation.
- Sora for display through title, Manrope for body and label, JetBrains Mono only for data.
- Motion is functional and short: print 400 ms, stamp 200 ms, all of it off under reduced motion.

## Colors

A cool, low-chroma navy system with exactly two warm voices: gold for action and paper for proof.

### Primary
- **Tender Gold** (proposed; `primary` and `cta`, dark): the action colour. Fills the filled button, the extended FAB and the wordmark tile; as `primary` it also carries text buttons, links, the focus ring, the NFC ring, progress and pager indicators. In light theme the fill stays gold (`cta`), while `primary` darkens to **Tender Gold Deep** (light-primary) so gold-coloured text still reads on the pale ground (6.5:1). The hex is a placeholder for UMak's gold until the team confirms the school's colours.

### Secondary
- **Carbon Copy Blue** (proposed; `secondary`, `secondary-container`): the quiet companion. Tonal buttons, the selected filter chip, the navigation indicator, the language toggle's active state, dialog icons and every avatar, including the profile monogram. Avatars use it precisely because gold means an action.

### Tertiary
- **Pending Ink Blue** (proposed; `tertiary-container`): the waiting state. Pending status chips, the "waiting for signal" banner, queued-receipt and assistant avatars.

### Status
- **Settled Green** (`success`, `success-container`): Settled chips, the Online chip, credited amounts (+₱).
- **Offline Amber** (`warning`, `warning-container`): the Offline chip, NFC-off and not-synced banners, the unlinked-ID notice.
- **Flag Red** (`error`, `error-container`): Failed and Flagged chips, the clone-suspect panel on /ops, failure banners.

### Neutral
- **Night Counter Navy** (proposed; `surface`, dark): the ground for every dark screen; the `surface-container-*` steps above it carry all depth.
- **Daylight Counter Grey** (proposed; light-surface): the light-theme ground.
- **Ledger Mist** (`on-surface-variant`, `outline`, `outline-variant`): supporting text, outlined button and chip strokes, list dividers.
- **Thermal Paper** (proposed; `paper`, dark; light-paper is plain white): the slip's stock. Used for nothing but slips.
- **Thermal Ink** (proposed; `on-paper`) and **Faded Thermal** (proposed; `paper-muted`): slip text, and the slip's secondary rows, dashed rules and receipt id (4.6:1 dark, 4.8:1 light).

### Stamp inks
- **Settled Stamp Green** (proposed; `stamp`) and **Pending Stamp Blue** (proposed; `stamp-pending`): stamp ink, identical in both themes because they only ever print on paper (5.7:1 and 6.3:1 on Thermal Paper; 6.5:1 and 7.2:1 on white).

### Named Rules
**The One Gold Rule.** A gold fill means "press here". It fills the filled button, the extended FAB and the wordmark tile, nothing else; avatars, decoration and illustration take Carbon Copy Blue.

**The Paper Is Proof Rule.** Paper and its inks appear only on the receipt slip. A surface painted paper-colour is claiming to be a receipt.

**The Placeholder Gold Rule.** The gold is a stand-in until UMak's colours are confirmed. When they land, change the seeds (`primary`, `primaryContainer` and their on-colours in both themes) and re-check every contrast pair; nothing else hardcodes gold.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sora (with Manrope, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Manrope (with system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, Consolas), data only
**Icon Font:** Material Symbols Rounded (weight 400, optical size 24, FILL 1 for the active nav item and status chips), self-hosted as a subset for the offline PWA

**Character:** Sora's geometric, slightly wide forms give amounts and titles a confident, printed-sign presence; Manrope keeps running text calm and legible at small sizes; JetBrains Mono is the till printer.

### Hierarchy
- **Hero** (700, 2.75rem rising to 4, 4.5 and 5.5rem at 600, 1200 and 1600 px, line-height 1.04, -0.03em): the landing h1 only. Never above 5.5rem.
- **Display** (display-m 600 at 2.8125rem; display-s 700 at 2.25rem; tabular figures): the amount being charged, loaded or available.
- **Headline** (600, 2rem / 1.75rem / 1.5rem): landing section heads, onboarding and login titles, dialog titles, keypad digits.
- **Title** (600, 1.375rem / 1rem / 0.875rem): top app bar titles, list headlines, card heads, list subheaders.
- **Body** (400, 1rem / 0.875rem / 0.75rem): explanations and supporting text; long copy held to 36 to 52ch.
- **Label** (600, 0.875rem / 0.75rem / 0.6875rem): buttons, chips, navigation labels, table heads. Sentence case, never uppercase except on the slip and stamp.
- **Data** (JetBrains Mono 400, 0.875rem rows, 0.75rem ids; TOTAL 600 at 1.5rem with tabular figures): slip rows, transaction hashes, receipt ids, student numbers, chip UIDs, timestamps in tables.

### Named Rules
**The Data Wears Mono Rule.** If a value could be copied into a block explorer or a ledger (amount on a slip, hash, receipt id, student number, UID), it is set in JetBrains Mono. Names, labels and sentences never are.

**The Tabular Amount Rule.** Every peso amount uses tabular figures so columns of amounts align.

## Layout

The app is a single centred column, 600 px max, on every window size; at 840 px and up it gains hairline side borders (`outline-variant`) so the column reads as a device on a desk. Below 600 px navigation is the bottom navigation bar (80); from 600 px it becomes an 80 px navigation rail on the left and the column shifts right. Screens pad 16 px at the sides; cards pad 16 px; dialogs 24 px.

The landing page uses a 1200 px container (1440 from 1600 px), gutters of 16 / 24 / 32 / 40 px at compact / medium / large / extra-large, and section padding of 56 / 72 / 96 / 112 px. The hero splits into copy plus a 560 px (640 from 1600) demo stage from 1200 px. /ops is the projector view: one column, then a 380 px rail plus fluid column from 1200 (440 from 1600), capped at 1760. Full-bleed bands stop at 1920 and centre.

Breakpoints follow the M3 window classes: medium 600, expanded 840, large 1200, extra-large 1600, wide 1920. Spacing is a 4 px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56, 72, 96, 112). Touch targets are at least 48 px. The M3 component and window-class numbers come from the published M3 spec but were not fetched and verified during the design session; check them before the build.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal. Surfaces step up through `surface-container-low` (cards, list groups) to `surface-container` (navigation, landing demo stage) to `surface-container-high` (dialogs, keypad keys, tap target) and `surface-container-highest` (text fields). The slip needs no shadow: warm paper on night navy separates at 16:1.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Level 3** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.45), 0 4px 10px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.3)` dark; `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.18), 0 4px 10px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.1)` light): the extended FAB, the snackbar and dialogs only.

### Named Rules
**The Tonal Depth Rule.** Cards, lists, slips and bars never cast a shadow. A shadow means the element floats above the page and will go away (FAB, snackbar, dialog).

## Shapes

M3 corners, tuned. Buttons and the navigation indicator are full pills (9999px); cards and keypad keys have gently rounded 12px corners; chips 8px; the extended FAB 16px; dialogs 28px; filled text fields round only the top corners (4px) and sit on a bottom stroke; the snackbar 4px. Avatars and the NFC tap target are circles.

The slip is the one shape that is not a rounded rectangle: square sides with zig-zag torn edges top and bottom, 14 px teeth, cut with a CSS conic-gradient mask. The stamp is a 6px-cornered box with a 3 px ink border, rotated -9 degrees. Dashed rules (1 px, Faded Thermal) divide the slip's header, rows and total.

## Components

### Buttons
Rounded pills, confident and plain.
- **Shape:** full pill (9999px).
- **Primary (filled):** Tender Gold fill (`cta`) with dark gold-brown text; 40 px tall with 24 px side padding, or 56 px with 28 px padding and title-m for the screen's main call to action (usually full width at the foot of the screen).
- **Hover / Focus / Pressed:** an M3 state layer of the text colour at 8% (hover) and 12% (pressed), 200 ms emphasized easing; keyboard focus is a 2 px `primary` outline offset 2 px.
- **Tonal:** Carbon Copy Blue container, for secondary actions such as demo shortcuts. **Outlined:** 1 px `outline` stroke, `primary` text, for Cancel and Log out. **Text:** `primary` text, 12 px padding, for dialog actions and low-emphasis links.

### Extended FAB
- One per screen, only for its single primary action (/me: Scan receipt). 56 px tall, 16px corners, gold fill, level-3 shadow, sits above the navigation bar (bottom 96 px) or 24 px from the bottom with the rail.

### Chips
- **Filter:** 32 px, 8px corners, 1 px outline stroke with `on-surface-variant` text; selected swaps to Carbon Copy Blue container with a leading check.
- **Status:** 28 px, 8px corners, label-m bold, always a filled icon plus a word: Settled (check_circle, success), Pending (schedule, tertiary), Failed (cancel, error), Flagged (flag, error).
- **Network:** 32 px, Online (cloud_done, success container) or Offline (cloud_off, warning container); lives in the top app bar's trailing slot.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** `surface-container-low`; banners use the matching `*-container` pair with a leading icon.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none; empty states use a 1 px dashed `outline-variant` border.
- **Internal Padding:** 16 px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** M3 filled field, 56 px, `surface-container-highest` fill, top corners 4px, 1 px `on-surface-variant` bottom stroke, body-s label above body-l value, optional leading icon and supporting text below.
- **Focus:** the bottom stroke thickens to 2 px and turns `primary`.
- **Assistant input:** a 56 px pill field with a 56 px gold send button beside it.

### Navigation
- **Top app bar:** 64 px, `surface`, back icon button (48 px) on the left, title-l Sora title, trailing actions (network chip, language toggle).
- **Navigation bar (below 600 px):** 80 px, `surface-container`, icon over label-m label; the active item sits in a 64 x 32 Carbon Copy Blue pill with a filled icon. /me: Home, Activity, Assistant, Profile. /pos: Charge, Today, Queue.
- **Navigation rail (600 px and up):** 80 px wide, same items stacked from the top, 56 x 32 indicator.
- **Language toggle:** EN / FIL segmented pill, 32 px, active segment in Carbon Copy Blue.

### Dialogs and Snackbar
- **Dialog:** 280 to 560 px wide, 28px corners, `surface-container-high`, 24 px padding, centred `secondary` icon, headline-s title, text-button actions right-aligned, over a 45% black scrim.
- **Snackbar:** `inverse-surface`, 4px corners, min 48 px, filled check icon, `inverse-primary` text action; enters 300 ms emphasized-decelerate.

### NFC Tap Target
- A 176 px circle in `surface-container-high` holding a 72 px contactless icon in `primary`, with a 2 px `primary` ring that pulses outward on a 1.6 s loop; headline-s instruction and body-l hint below.

### Receipt Slip (signature)
The thermal receipt every payment becomes.
- **Stock:** Thermal Paper (`paper`) with Thermal Ink text, 20 px side and 28 px top/bottom padding, zig-zag torn top and bottom edges.
- **Content, top to bottom:** stall name (Sora title-m bold, centred); "HeronPay receipt" with date and time (mono body-s, Faded Thermal); dashed rule; mono rows of item and amount, then "Paid by" with the masked ID in Faded Thermal; dashed rule; TOTAL (mono body-m) against the amount (mono headline-s 600, tabular); optional QR (192 px, always black on white) on the POS Approved slip; receipt id (mono label-s, centred).
- **Where:** POS Approved (with QR), student Receipt (pending and settled), the landing demo, and the third onboarding page. Lists of payments stay M3 list items with status chips.
- **Motion:** on Approved the slip prints in by clipping from the top, 400 ms, emphasized-decelerate `cubic-bezier(.05,.7,.1,1)`.

### Stamp (signature)
- **SETTLED** in Settled Stamp Green with "block" and the block number beneath in mono; **PENDING** in Pending Stamp Blue with "waiting for signal" beneath.
- Sora 700, 18 px, 0.08em tracking, uppercase, 3 px ink border, 6px corners, rotated -9 degrees, on a 70% paper wash, anchored 16 px from the right and 30 px from the bottom.
- **Placement:** the slip reserves 96 px of bottom padding whenever it carries a stamp; the stamp sits in that space and never covers a row of text.
- **Motion:** Settled lands with a 200 ms drop from 1.6x scale to 1x, emphasized easing.

### Named Rules
**The Reserved Stamp Rule.** A stamp only appears where the slip has made room for it. If a stamp would overlap text, the slip grows; the stamp never moves up.

**The Honest Stamp Rule.** SETTLED appears only once the chain has confirmed the payment and always carries the block number; until then the slip says PENDING.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** show a payment as one object on the receipt slip (POS Approved, student Receipt, landing demo, onboarding page 3), and keep payment lists as 72 px M3 list items with status chips.
- **Do** reserve 96 px at the foot of any stamped slip and anchor the stamp inside it.
- **Do** set amounts on slips, hashes, receipt ids, student numbers and UIDs in JetBrains Mono, with tabular figures for every peso amount.
- **Do** pair every Pending, Settled, Failed and Flagged state with a word and a filled icon.
- **Do** give avatars, including the profile monogram, Carbon Copy Blue (`secondary-container`); gold is for action.
- **Do** keep the QR code black on white in both themes.
- **Do** design every screen in dark (default) and light, holding 4.5:1 for text in both, paper and stamp pairs included.
- **Do** use M3 emphasized easing `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)`: screen changes 300 ms, status changes 200 ms; and turn all animation and transitions off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** label every sample name, stall, transaction and stat with the dashed "Sample" tag until real data exists.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill anything gold that is not an action (decorative circles, chat bubbles, icon tiles); the wordmark tile is the only exception.
- **Don't** paint any non-receipt surface in paper colours.
- **Don't** let a stamp cover slip text, or stamp SETTLED without a block number.
- **Don't** draw the balance as a bank card or wallet card with gradients; it is a plain tonal card.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards, slips, lists or bars; level-3 shadow is for the FAB, snackbar and dialogs only.
- **Don't** set names, labels or sentences in mono.
- **Don't** put eyebrow labels above headings or thick coloured side borders on cards.
- **Don't** take the landing hero above 5.5rem.
